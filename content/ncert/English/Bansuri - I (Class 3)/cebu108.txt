are syllables that can be used. For

tha dhi dhom num.

num dhom dhi tha.

tha dhi dhom num num
dhom dhi tha.

tha tha dhi dhidhom dhom
num num.

Try saying these in rhythm with
claps or dandia sticks or any other
instruments in the school.
