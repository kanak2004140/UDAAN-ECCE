ACTIVITY 11    Group Role Plays
The class is divided into groups of 
5 or 6. Each group gets a simple 
story. Children prepare, practice 
and perform!
Previously, you were just two actors in the scene. 
Now we add more! Though what you individually 
do is the same expression, action and speech, 
the process is entirely different. Does the scene 
get better working in groups? Does it cause more 
trouble? No way … the more the merrier!
Familiar stories 
can be derived from 
various lessons in 
other textbooks, 
e.g., language 
books, history 
books, etc.
Themes can be derived from  
epics, legends, Tenali Rama, 
Panchatantra or other literature. 
Example— The clever crow, 
Ekalavya, the cat’s judgement, Arjuna’s focus 
during training, Shravana Kumara or  
Ganesha’s devotion to Shiva and Parvati.
