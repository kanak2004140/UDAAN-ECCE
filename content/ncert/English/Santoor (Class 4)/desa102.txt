We use different forms of adjectives to compare the 
qualities of people or things.
• 
When we compare the qualities of two people or objects, 
we add -er to the adjective. We always use ‘than’ while 
comparing two people or objects.
	
Example- Aman is shorter than Jiya.
• 
When the comparison is made in a whole group or 
collection, we add -est to the adjective. 
	
Example- February is the shortest month of the year.
