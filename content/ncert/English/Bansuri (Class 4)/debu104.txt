Chapter 4
PEOPLE IN ACTION
Recall a time when you were in a 
crowded place with people moving 
around in different positions and 
actions. Each person’s posture and 
movement could tell a different story.
Nandalal Bose’s paintings in Chapter 1 
show a variety of postures and actions 
of people.
In this chapter, you will learn to use 
geometric shapes to sketch people in 
action.
Inspired by traditional Indian 
toys, you will craft geometric 
3D forms to make a spinning 
doll and enjoy playing with it.
Get ready to imagine, create, 
and have fun!

Activity 4.2
How will you draw people when 
they are moving?

body, you will see that it is made 
of spherical and cylindrical forms.

Activity 4.4
Follow the instructions to create a spinning 
doll that you can play with.
Step 1: Draw the shapes of the required 
size on your paper. You can make them 
bigger too.
Step 2: Colour the shapes if you are using 
a plain paper.
Step 3: Cut out the shapes. Now make the 
body parts from each shape.
•	 A thin stick or broom stick
•	 A5 size paper (half A4) plain/coloured
•	 Glue
•	 Pens/sketch pens
•	 Crayons/paints to colour (optional)
