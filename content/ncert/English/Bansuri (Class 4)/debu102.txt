Chapter 2
TEXTURES IN NATURE
Many stories from the Panchatantra, 
Hitopadesha, and Jataka tales are set in 
jungles. Nature is full of incredible shapes, 
forms, colours, patterns, and textures. 
Textures can be felt through touch–rough, 
smooth, bumpy, soft, and everything in 
between. 
In this chapter, you will step outside 
to explore the textures in your natural 
surroundings. 
Then you will create your own 
Kalpavriksha or wish-fulfilling tree by 
learning techniques like:
•	 Frottage—rubbing on textured 
surfaces to capture their patterns.
•	 Collage—cutting and pasting pieces of 
paper, leaf, etc., on a surface.
•	 Leaf printing—painting the surface of 
a leaf and pressing it on a surface.

Activity 2.1
Imagine a Wish-fulfilling Tree
Look at the trees around you, or pictures of trees, and 
discuss the following:
•	 Their size—are they tall, short, big or small?
•	 Their parts—trunk, branches, leaves, flowers, fruits, 
thorns, roots, etc.
•	 What kinds of lines, shapes, colours, and textures do 
you see?
Imagine that you are in a magical forest, where you can 
make your own Kalpavriksha or wish-fulfilling tree!
Trees give us so many things—oxygen to breath, fruits, 
shade and so much.
•	 What kind of tree would you like to make? 
•	 It can be of any size, shape, or colour.
•	 Sketch two to three ideas for your tree.

Activity 2.2
Frottage
The process of capturing textures by 
rubbing on surfaces is called frottage. 
You can use pencils, colour pencils, or 
crayons for this. 
Gather your materials for the activity and 
step outside.
Touch and explore different textures
•	 Touch different surfaces, such as soil, 
pebbles, stones, leaves, flowers, tree 
barks, etc.
•	 Does the texture of a bark feel the same 
as that of a leaf?
•	 Observe the lines, shapes, and colours 
of different textures.
•	 Compare the kinds of line patterns each 
texture creates.
•	 Observe the lines on the leaves, shown 
in Chapter 4 of The World Around Us 
textbook.
Make your frottage by following the 
instructions given on the next page.

Frottage Process
1. Place a sheet of paper on your chosen 
surface.
2. Hold the sheet on the surface and rub a 
coloured pencil or a crayon on it.
3. Observe the texture that is captured.
4.  Rub it with a little more pressure to 
capture more details.
5. Place the sheet on different surfaces 
and repeat the steps.
Gather all your frottage artworks for the 
next activity.

Activity 2.3
Collage is the process of pasting different 
materials on any surface to make an 
artwork.
Collage Process
1. Prepare a large surface by joining sheets 
of paper. You can use old newspapers or 
a large piece of waste fabric.
2. Create the outline of your wish-fulfilling 
tree by drawing its trunk and branches 
based on your preparatory sketches.
3. Gather the frottage work (you can also 
include 
materials 
having 
different 
textures).
4. Select textures from your frottage work 
by sharing and exchanging with your 
friends.
5. Arrange and paste the frottage work 
within the outlines of the trunk and 
branches.
Collage of Trunk and Branches

Activity 2.4
After completing the collage, add 
leaves to the branches of your tree.
Leaf-printing Process
1. Gather leaves of different shapes, 
sizes and textures.
2. Mix some paint and water to create 
a creamy paste.
3. Apply the paste on the leaf with 
your finger, a sponge or a brush.
4. Place it on the desired area and 
press it hard.
5. Lift it off the surface carefully.
6. Repeat this process with different 
leaves and colours.
7. Create patterns and arrangements 
while you print the leaves.
Complete your tree and invite your 
friends to make wishes.
Note for Teachers: 
This can also be
done in small groups 
of 4 to 5 students.
