DANCES AROUND ME
Every region in our country has a unique 
culture and tradition. It also has its unique 
music and dance.

Many of our local dances are connected to 
nature.
For example, you learned a Kannada song in 
music class, “Aadona banni kanna muchale 
tugona banni uyyale.” The song describes 
trees, birds, and the nature. Or you can 
choose nature-related songs, like Rabindra 
Sangeet’s 
“Phule 
phule 
dhole 
dhole…” 
describing flowers, rivers, and birds.
Put together all the dance steps with gentle 
and vigorous movements, hand gestures, and 
facial expressions and dance to the rhythm 
of the song.

Learning My Regional Dance
Note for Teachers: 
Taking the students to a performance of a 
traditional dance form will help, them in 
understanding the different elements of the 
particular dance. This can be discussed in 
class to let the children share their thoughts 
and feelings about the dance performance.
