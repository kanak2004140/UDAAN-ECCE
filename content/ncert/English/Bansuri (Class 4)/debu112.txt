Understanding Music
When songs have words and lyrics, 
we can easily understand what they 
are trying to convey. But when the 
song is played only with instruments, 
it can be trickier to understand what 
it is about.
Interpreting Music
Listen to a piece of instrumental 
music, or the one given in the

•	 Close your eyes and try to imagine a picture or 
a scene that is represented by the music.
•	 Can you describe the feeling or the mood of 
the music? Now, listen to the music again, and 
imagine a story that matches the music. Write
