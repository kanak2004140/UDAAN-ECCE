Chapter 3
AQUA WORLD
Aqua is another word for 
water. Aquarium starts with 
this word. Aqua is also used 
to describe some shades of 
colour, such as aqua blue 
or aqua green.
Dive into this chapter and 
let your imagination flow.
Observe patterns as you 
draw, colour, and create 
many aquatic creatures.
Twist and turn lines into 
animal forms, as you create 
new designs of your own.
Construct hanging 
artworks called mobiles, 
that move.
What are you waiting for? 
Put on your art goggles 
and dive right in with a 
wondrous splash!
